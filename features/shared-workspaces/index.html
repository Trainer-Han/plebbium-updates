Shared workspaces

StableNew in Betasince 3612

Share a workspace and it stays the same for everyone in it, live: its tabs, tab groups, name and notes. See who is on which tab, follow someone, or point a tab out to the others.

How to use it

  1. Share: right-click a workspace's icon › Share…, then send the invite link.
  2. Join: open the invite link. People without Plebbium can follow along on a view-only page in any browser.
  3. Point out: right-click a tab › Point out, or the Point out button beside the address bar. The tab flashes for the others, with Go there.
  4. Follow someone: you go along to whichever tab they are on.
  5. Keep a tab to yourself: right-click it › Keep this tab private. Pinned tabs never leave your PC either.
  6. The owner chooses who else may invite, and can remove anyone. Leaving closes the workspace and its tabs on your PC.

Why it works this way

A relay that can't read what it passes on

Every change is locked on your PC with the workspace's key before it leaves, and the key travels in the invite link after the #, a part of an address browsers never send to a server. The relay at sync.plebbian.com only passes locked data along.

Your logins stay yours

Logins, cookies, passwords and history never leave your PC. Everyone in a shared workspace uses their own normal logins, so each person is signed in as themselves.

Removing someone changes the lock

When someone is removed, the workspace gets a new key, handed to each remaining member alone, and old invite links stop working.
