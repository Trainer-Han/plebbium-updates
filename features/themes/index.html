Themes

Stablesince 3612

Plebbium's look in violet, or the same look in red: Pleb's own pages and the browser's tabs and toolbar follow.

How to use it

  1. Plebbium settings › Appearance › Theme: Violet or Red.
  2. Pages change at once; Search with Plebbium follows from the next start.

When it arrived

  • 156.0.8078.361222 Oct 2026 · Beta and Stable

    The red theme, beside violet.

Why it works this way

One seed colour

The browser derives its colours from one seed, so changing it recolours the tabs, toolbar and menus together.

Colours that mean something stay

Green for on, red for danger and the like keep their colours in both themes.
