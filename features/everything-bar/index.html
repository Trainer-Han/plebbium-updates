Everything bar

Stablesince 3604

One box in the middle of the window for everything: commands, open tabs, bookmarks, history, the Archive, highlights, sums and conversions. A question asks ChatGPT, Claude or Pleb AI.

How to use it

  1. Open it: Ctrl+Shift+A, or the Pleb menu › Everything bar.
  2. Type anything: commands, tab titles, bookmarks and history come up together.
  3. Narrow it with a prefix: > commands, % tabs, * bookmarks, / history, ~ the Archive, " highlights, @ workspaces.
  4. Sums and conversions: start with =.
  5. Ask: ? asks Pleb AI on this computer, gpt ChatGPT, clau Claude; a plain question asks ChatGPT on Enter.

When it arrived

  • 156.0.8078.360418 Oct 2026 · Beta and Stable

Why it works this way

In the middle, where it doesn't move

It opens near the top of the page area, centred, with the window dimmed behind it: where a command bar belongs, and where it stays put as results come and go.

A window pages can't fake

It belongs to the browser, not to a page, so no website can see it, draw over it or pretend to be it.

Quick to open

Its key belongs to the browser itself, so the bar opens at once, without waiting for anything to wake up.
