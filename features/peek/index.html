Peek

Stablesince 3604

Hold Alt and click a link: it opens in a floating window over the page you're on. Keep it as a tab, or click away and it's gone.

How to use it

  1. Hold Alt and click a link.
  2. Read it in the floating window; your page stays where it was.
  3. Keep it with Open as tab, or click outside it to close it.
  4. Its size: Plebbium settings › Peek.

When it arrived

  • 156.0.8078.360418 Oct 2026 · Beta and Stable

    Peek.

Why it works this way

Only for its own tab

A page can ask for a peek only for itself, never for another tab, so a website can't use it to pop things up elsewhere.

A glance, not a commitment

Many links are read once: a peek saves opening, reading and closing a tab.
