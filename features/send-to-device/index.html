Send to device

Betasince 3613

Send a tab, a link or selected text to your phone or your other PCs: into the workspace open there, or a shared workspace that PC joined. A phone pairs with a QR code and gets a Plebbium page on its home screen, no app, and sends back.

How to use it

  1. Pair a phone or PC: Plebbium settings › Your devices › Add a device, then scan the QR code. A PC already in your group says yes.
  2. Send: right-click a page, link, tab or selected text › Send to device, or the button in the address bar.
  3. Receive on a PC: a notice with Open, and Sent to you in the Pleb menu.
  4. Send back from a phone: its Plebbium page, or Android's Share sheet.

When it arrived

Why it works this way

No Google sync needed

Chromium's own sending runs on Google's sync, which a browser like Plebbium can't offer everyone. Plebbium's works for everyone, through Plebbium's own relay.

Encrypted end to end

Everything sent is locked with your devices' shared key; the relay only holds locked data, deletes it once collected, and keeps nothing more than 7 days.

No app

A phone uses a page added to its home screen, so Chrome on Android or an iPhone's browser is enough.
