Highlights

Stablesince 3604

Select text on any page and highlight it, with a note if you like. The highlight is there again when you come back, and every highlight is searchable from the Everything bar.

How to use it

  1. Select text, right-click › Highlight the selected text, or Highlight with note….
  2. Come back to the page: your highlights are there.
  3. Find them: " in the Everything bar, or Highlights in the sidebar.

When it arrived

  • 156.0.8078.360418 Oct 2026 · Beta and Stable

    Highlights.

Why it works this way

Kept on your computer

Highlights are kept by Pleb's service on your own computer, beside your reading history, not in a cloud.

A page sees only its own

A page can only ask for the highlights on that page, for its own tab.
