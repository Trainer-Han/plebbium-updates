What you would get

Click the Pleb button and pick What's in Plebbium: this page opens in a new tab, with every feature Plebbium has, how to use each one, and what is in Beta and planned. Nothing is sent: it is an ordinary public page.

For example

Someone asks how to keep their work and home logins apart. Pleb button › What's in Plebbium › Workspaces shows them where to click.

Why it works this way
