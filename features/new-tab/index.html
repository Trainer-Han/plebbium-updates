New tab

StableNew in Betasince 3604

A calm new tab: a clock, one search box with predictions as you type and a search button, !bangs, your top sites, pick up where you left off, and a background picture of your own, dimmed to taste.

How to use it

  1. Search: type, then pick a prediction or press Enter or the search button.
  2. Search a site directly: start with a !bang.
  3. A background picture: Customize, bottom right › choose one from your computer, then set how dim it is.
  4. Predictions off: Plebbium settings › New tab.
  5. A plain white clock: Plebbium settings › Appearance › White clock. Customize › Reset to default takes the picture away and goes back to Violet.

Why it works this way

Predictions send only what you type

The one thing on the new tab that uses the network: only the box's text goes, without cookies, and never an address, a bang or a question for an AI.

Your picture, kept on your PC

Only your own picture, never one fetched from the web; it is resized and kept on that PC.
