Split view

Stablesince 3604

Two tabs side by side in one window: Chromium's own split view, opened from the Everything bar.

How to use it

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+A, type split and choose the split command.
  2. Split tabs are never put to sleep or archived by Calm tabs.

When it arrived

  • 156.0.8078.360418 Oct 2026 · Beta and Stable

    Split view, from the Everything bar.

Why it works this way

Chromium's own

Chromium has a split view of its own, so Plebbium opens it rather than copying it, and gets every fix Chromium makes to it.
