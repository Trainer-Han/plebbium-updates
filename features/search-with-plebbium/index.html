Search with Plebbium

Stablesince 3604

Search from anywhere in Windows: Plebbium's tray icon, its shortcut or its hotkey opens a search box on the desktop, dark glass with the start window's glow.

How to use it

  1. Click Plebbium's tray icon, or open the Search with Plebbium shortcut.
  2. Type and press Enter: the result opens in Plebbium, your last session restored first.

Why it works this way

Chromium's own, in Plebbium's look

It is Chromium's own desktop search, restyled: always dark, like the start window, with the Plebbium logo. How it searches is untouched.

Plebbium keeps running

With it on, closing every window isn't quitting: Plebbium waits in the tray, and a search brings back your session and its workspaces.
