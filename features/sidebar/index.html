Sidebar

Stablesince 3605

A strip of icons at the right edge of the window: Pleb's tools on top, the sites you pin below. Each opens docked beside the page, signed in with your normal logins, and sites keep running while closed.

How to use it

  1. Show or hide it: Alt+Shift+S, the button at its foot, the Pleb menu, or sidebar in the Everything bar.
  2. Add a site: the + in the strip, or right-click a tab › Add to sidebar.
  3. Right-click a site for its mobile layout, sound, blocker switch and more; drag icons to reorder them.
  4. Volume: sidebar sites play at the sidebar's level, 50% to start. The speaker in a site's panel turns it: scroll over it, or click it to give that site a volume of its own.
  5. Hidden but handy: let it peek in at the window's edge (a switch in the Pleb menu).

Why it works this way

Real pages, not frames

A sidebar site is a page of its own, like a tab, never a frame inside another page: sites refuse to be framed, and a framed page wouldn't have your logins, permission prompts or dialogs.

Phone-sized by default

Sites open in their phone layout, which fits a narrow panel, unless they send phones to an app store; those open as they do on a computer.

Volume in the engine

The sidebar's volume works on every site and in every player because the browser turns the sound down itself, without the page knowing.
